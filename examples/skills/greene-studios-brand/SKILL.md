# Greene Studios brand

Follow the Greene Studios identity in everything you generate or review:

- Primary color `#1E4D2B`, accent `#D1FF5C`, surface `#FCFAF8`.
- Headlines: confident and editorial, 5 words or fewer when possible.
- Body copy: short sentences, no hype words, no exclamation marks.
- Layouts: generous whitespace, 8px spacing system, 12–20px corner radii.
- Buttons: dark green fill with the lime accent reserved for one primary action per screen.

When reviewing designs, check brand-color consistency before anything else.
